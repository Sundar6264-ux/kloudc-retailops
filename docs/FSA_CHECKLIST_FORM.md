Description: "Complete all closing tasks before leaving. Ensure the kitchen and front service area are clean,
stocked, organized, and ready for the next day's service."

1. * Chef Name (dropdown): Amandeep, Harshitha, RK, Sadia, Other
2. Section "Turn off Heat" (Turn off all heat sources, including Food Stand Stoves, Burners, Fryers, and other
   Cooking Equipment.)
3. * Have you Turned Off? (grid, each row required; columns Yes / No / Not Applicable):
   Stoves of the Hot Food Stand; Panini / Paratha Warmer; Flavored Rice Cooker; Snacks/Samosa Stand;
   Chaat Rice Cooker; Both Fryers; All Stoves; Oven; "Open" board light
4. * Wrap up Leftover Veggies (grid, each row required; columns Done / Not Applicable):
   Trash old and mushy veggies; Consolidate remaining into one container and get them ready for next day in
   Chaat station
5. * Wrap Up Leftovers & Sauce Bottles (checkboxes):
   Discard old or mushy vegetables; Consolidate remaining vegetables into one container and keep ready for next
   day (Chaat station); Wrap up leftover proteins, biryani, sweets and other food items properly and keep them in
   cold storage appropriately for next day use; Clean sauce bottles and move them to the main bain-marie; Safely
   wrap Parathas, Breads, Naans and buns and clean the area; Check with the chef before discarding any undated
   items in the cooler.
6. * Clean up the entire area (checkboxes):
   Outside Kitchen's customer area, Food stand see through glass & Counter top; Sweets Cooler (Wipe off inside
   and outside); Rice Cooker (Wipe off inside and outside); Chaat Cooker (Wipe off inside and outside); Samosa
   Warmer (Wipe off inside and outside); Chaat Station (Wipe off inside and outside); Food Stand (empty the
   water in container wells and move ice bags into frontside glass door freezer); Panini / Paratha Machine and
   the area; Printer table; Surface of the Trash can next to printer
7. * Re-organize Supplies and display boards and cutlery (checkboxes):
   Pack and organize all packaging and cutlery supplies neatly.; Inform chef about any shortage of supplies
8. * Is there anything unusual? (like shortage of supplies, technical or mechanical issues etc.) (paragraph)
